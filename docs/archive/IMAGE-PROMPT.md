## Final prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: premium cinematic editorial web artwork for a university computer engineering collective, no typography. Primary request: an extraordinary photoreal CGI museum installation: a gigantic sculptural pixel T-Rex dinosaur assembled from precision-machined reflective brushed platinum cubes, perched on a monumental angular black basalt plinth in an infinite dark forest-green architectural void. The creature is the familiar elegant side-profile pixel dinosaur silhouette, but extruded as a substantial heavy three-dimensional object with beveled cube edges. Some cubes detach from its back and tail and float in an exquisitely art-directed arc. Tiny delicate golden electronic traces appear between some cubes. Materials are real machined silver metal with subtle green reflections, some polished and some brushed, very high microdetail. The floor is dark wet stone with a quiet realistic reflection. Massive narrow vertical ivory light slit in the distant background, mist and sparse dust motes. Cinematic architectural photography, 35mm lens, low angle, dramatic rim lighting and sophisticated chiaroscuro. Composition: ultra-wide 16:9 landscape, entire sculpture contained in central 70%, generous dark margins, dramatic negative space, depth, monumental scale. Color palette: near-black forest green, platinum silver, restrained antique gold, never purple/neon-blue. Extremely refined contemporary experimental design studio aesthetic, physically based Octane/Cinema4D quality, surreal but plausible installation. No letters, words, watermark, UI, text, people, or cartoon rendering. Not a cute toy; an awe-inspiring technological artifact. Output high resolution landscape.
